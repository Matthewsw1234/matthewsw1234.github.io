JARVIS

A personal AI assistant operated by Matthew Wilkins.

JARVIS is Matthew Wilkins's private, personal assistant. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public. It helps Matthew Wilkins manage his own calendar, email, reminders and projects, and it answers his assistant phone number when he is unavailable.

Phone and text messaging

JARVIS has a phone number that people may choose to call or text to reach Matthew Wilkins. When someone contacts that number, JARVIS replies conversationally, explains that it is an AI assistant, offers to take a message, and passes the message on to Matthew Wilkins.

How people opt in: only by contacting the number themselves. JARVIS never sends messages to anyone who has not first texted or called it, and it never sends marketing. The first reply to a new texter identifies the sender and explains how to opt out. Message frequency varies with the conversation. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

Example conversation

Person: Hi, is Matthew around?

JARVIS: Hi, this is JARVIS, Matthew's AI assistant. He can't reply right now. Who's this, and can I take a message?

JARVIS, AI assistant for Matthew. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Person: It's Dave, can he call me back about Tuesday?

JARVIS: Thanks Dave, I'll make sure Matthew gets that.

JARVIS also sends alerts (forwarded messages and reminders) to Matthew Wilkins's own mobile phone, which he set up himself.
