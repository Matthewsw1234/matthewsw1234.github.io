Privacy Policy

JARVIS, operated by Matthew Wilkins · Last updated November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information JARVIS (the personal assistant service operated by Matthew Wilkins) collects when you call or text its phone number, and how that information is used.

Information we collect

When you contact JARVIS, it receives your phone number, the content of your text messages, a text transcript of what you say on a call, and any details you choose to share (such as your name and the message you want to leave). JARVIS does not record call audio.

How it is used

This information is used only to reply to you, to take your message, and to pass your message on to Matthew Wilkins. It is not used for advertising or marketing of any kind.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

To provide the service, messages pass through service providers that process them on Matthew Wilkins's behalf and only for that purpose: Twilio (phone and text delivery) and Anthropic (the AI model that writes JARVIS's replies).

Retention

Messages you leave are kept in Matthew Wilkins's private records until he deletes them. You can ask for your messages to be deleted at any time by emailing mswilkins02@gmail.com.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any text from JARVIS to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help.
